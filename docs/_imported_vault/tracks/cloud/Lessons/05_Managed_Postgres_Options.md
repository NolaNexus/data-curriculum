# Managed Postgres options (personal projects)

For learning and small projects, choose something with a clear free/cheap entry point:
- Supabase free plan (published limits). citeturn0search3
- Neon free plan + plan details. citeturn1search0turn1search17
- Render has pricing including a free tier listing for some services. citeturn1search2

**Rule:** do not migrate to managed DB until you’ve passed G2 (idempotency),
otherwise you’ll pay money to reproduce your own bugs.
