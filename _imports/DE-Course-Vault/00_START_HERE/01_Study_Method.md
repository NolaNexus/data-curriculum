# Study method (keeps you honest)

## Completeness for *this* project
“Complete” means the vault reliably produces the North Star outcome—not that it lists every topic.

It’s complete enough when:
- every North Star step has **lesson + lab + checks**
- the **order** is obvious and prerequisites are explicit
- feedback loops exist (tests, rubrics, reviews, debug logs)
- you can explain + reproduce your work later

## Default session loop (45–90 minutes)
1. Lesson: skim + write 5 bullet summary
2. Lab: do one concrete step (produce an artifact)
3. Capture: failure notes in `12_Troubleshooting/`
4. Commit: push to your repo (even small)

## Weekly loop (20 minutes)
- Fill `09_Templates/Weekly_Review_Template.md`
- Update `03_Progress_Board.md`
